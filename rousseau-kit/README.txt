Refined Rousseau-inspired models

Files: rousseau-models.blend; lion.glb; sleeping-person.glb; stringed-instrument.glb; sleeping-gypsy-three-models.glb; scene-preview.png.

Revision: narrower lion torso, modeled shoulder and haunch muscles, fuller mane with fine tapered strands, smaller eyes and ears, fuller muzzle bridge; continuous garment stripes with modeled cloth folds; added nose, nostril, cheek and ear detail; instrument rosette inlays, fret markers and bridge pins.

These remain stylized approximations of the painting rather than photorealistic scans. Fixed poses, no skeletons or animation. Colors and geometry are embedded in the GLB files. The combined GLB preserves the arrangement; individual models have local origins. The Blender file also includes the studio floor, camera and lighting. Babylon.js import is not live-tested.

The previous version remains in the neighboring rousseau-models folder.
